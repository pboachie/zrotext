# Android accepted profile03 manifest history

This additive source path preserves the first-only profile03 transport and the original ten
registration/activation/election evidence fields. It does not create an account root, reader,
signer, physical selection or conversation permission. Server support and independent end-to-end
qualification remain separate requirements.

An authenticated current phone connection first reads the immutable original evidence through
`payload_profile_manifest_original03_request`. Its closed reply also contains the actual selected
binding UUID. The phone compares that association with its retained exact E500 reservation and
device proof, independently verifies the original root, registration, activation and signatures,
and reads the existing nonexportable hardware keys. A missing acceptance response may complete
only that same already signed local row under the existing atomic old-binding, closure and
unresolved-effect fences. This is historical completion and restores no physical capability.

The phone derives the exact original ancestry prefix and requests a fixed current snapshot with
the separate manifest-history protocol. Each page contains at most eight entries and 64 KiB of
raw entry material. Every entry retains its exact 513-byte possession statement, received low-S
signature, exact root-signed successor and original acceptance time. The phone checks same-root
N+1 linkage, selected binding/election/origin, genuine role5 possession, the narrow retained-record
policy and every prefix commitment. Acceptance is historical input from the authenticated read;
it does not supply current permission. A changed tip, gap, fork, substituted original or partial
chain grants no availability. There is no lifetime renewal limit or history compaction.

A separate Room file retains all protected originals and monotonic progress. The established
enrollment protection key is used with nonempty domain-bound AAD; missing keys or schema refuse
recovery instead of creating a replacement or dropping data. Cold verification streams at most
eight retained records per worker chunk. A cold installed phase or old readback row cannot create
a live manifest handle. After a complete verified tip is prepared durably, an exact nonce-matched
readback on the actual current full device lease must match its version, semantic digest and full
ancestry prefix before the new tuple can be published.

The shipping full consumer uses `device_manifest_selection03` and `message_permission03` with
their required current manifest and ancestry prefix. It never rewrites those kinds into the old
first-only selection grammar. Actual present owner/session permission comes only from each
message's admitted intent and current source. Historical physical refresh evidence is checked
under its original manifest and acceptance/install windows, while the complete accepted chain
preserves the same physical reader identity and the actual current opaque SIM lease. A new
connection still needs a genuine fresh phone review and owner reapproval; stored observations
and acknowledgements cannot recreate that lease.

New-tip delivery withdraws old conversation RAM lifetimes before queued key or Room work. Old
content intervals, attempts and receipts remain exact and are not translated to a new manifest.
Read requests share a bounded per-connection budget, fresh correlation and a minimum interval;
interruption pauses the read and preserves its durable progress. The explicit **Check and resume
messaging setup** action selects a fresh authenticated snapshot without resetting pairing,
keys, history, request budgets or unknown signature outcomes. Ready remains reader availability;
every message still needs its own current grant, consent and effect checks.
