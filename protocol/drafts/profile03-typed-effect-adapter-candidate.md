# Typed effect adapter candidate

This server source candidate connects the closed typed command codecs to their
distinct storage capability and authenticated device-stream transport. Source,
schema vectors and authored tests do not establish Android journal execution,
platform entry, hardware custody, carrier delivery or permission to enable a
deployment. The ordinary stock-role adapter journeys still require execution.

The device-stream handler retains the signing point verified by the actual
socket challenge and session claim. A later public-key SELECT, UUID, received
Ready frame or role-signing key field cannot replace that witness. Each NEW
grant atomically retains its original enrolled point and established key ID,
original full-session digest and exact Fetch362 commitment. Grant and fetch
readers compare those retained originals with the actual current selection.
Old grants without that typed actor ledger cannot acquire new effects through
the older grant-schema capability.

Typed frames have their own closed parser and never become a legacy
`radio_event`. The recognized request kinds are `payload_profile_submit03`,
`payload_profile_outcome03`, `payload_profile_submit_status03_request`,
`payload_profile_no_submit_challenge03_request`,
`payload_profile_no_submit_proof03` and
`payload_profile_no_submit_status03_request`. The separate passive clock kind
is described in [the clock candidate](profile03-history-clock-candidate.md).
Requests retain the ordinary four-KiB bound. The existing current/content and
legacy parsers keep their own exact fieldsets and kinds.

Recognized requests first spend the shared typed-frame budget under the actual
current enrolled actor in an independently committed native transaction.
Closure challenge and proof requests also spend ordinary management admission;
passive status and clock use ordinary read admission. Submit and outcome traffic
do not receive an additional management cap. A separate business transaction
re-enters the native/current actor scope and independently rechecks the original
attempt. Malformed recognized bodies, failed proofs and later refusals cannot
roll back the already committed request-admission debit.

A NEW submit requires the complete held current owner, original consent,
manifest ancestry, physical selection, mapping, billing, signed ceiling and
original grant deadline. The independently verified socket point must still
match the original retained point and locked current enrolled key. Only the
first actual submit INSERT followed by successful COMMIT can produce
`submit_once` with its distinct submit-current and interval projections. Exact
duplicate commands produce `history_only` with null permission fields. Lost
COMMIT responses remain UNKNOWN; an exact retry or passive status never
reconstructs another invocation permit. A local drain during COMMIT withholds
the first response while retaining the committed original.

Outcome, closure and passive status use a separate current original-enrolled
actor scope. They do not require the old owner session, content, physical handle
or effect-health flag. Their immutable command still binds the original grant,
submit event, Fetch362 and full-session commitment. NEW outcome assertion times
come from the distinct fresh passive clock; raw callback wall times remain
separate on the phone. Historical status timestamps are never fresh clocks.
Current activation-version1 and version2 history leases retain their exact
established digest framing without broadening the version2 content parser.

The phone must permanently exclude platform entry through its own same-writer
pre-entry closing barrier and final closure CAS before requesting a signed
no-submit challenge. The server verifies the original enrolled-key signature
over that exact challenge and original closed record under current actor locks.
An ACK-authorized closed record additionally requires the actual prior committed
submit. This is authenticated device-reported durable closure, not remote OS
attestation. An unsigned assertion, absence of callbacks, elapsed time or an
ambiguous entry phase cannot release busy. Accepted closure releases only the
device-busy resource and retains quota, grant/attempt and replay tombstones;
it never refunds, requeues, rearms or creates a replacement attempt. Contrary
later evidence follows the separate typed quarantine policy without putting a
terminal old fence back into the active-device index.

Legacy timers, cancellation and event batches exclude typed profile03 work;
their existing ordinary message behavior is independent. No legacy callback or
permission brand can authorize the new typed submit. Android's actual durable
phase driver, callback classification, passive uploader and full transport
journeys remain separate implementation and qualification boundaries.
