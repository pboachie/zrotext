ZROtext device-status observer

Device status

This read-only view shows gateway device status for the account that invited you. It never shows message content, recipients, or credentials.

Accept an invitation

Paste the invitation token your account owner gave you and choose your own password. The invitation works once and is bound to your email address.

Sign in

Use your verified observer email and password.

Verify your email

Enter the code from your ZROtext verification email together with the password you chose. You must verify before you can sign in.

Gateway devices

An authenticated socket lease is a server observation that can lag a dropped connection by up to 90 seconds. It does not prove SMS permission, SIM service, or dispatch readiness. Pending and in-flight counts are writer states, not sendability.

    Change your password

    Changing your password signs out this and every other observer session.
