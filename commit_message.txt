⚡ Bolt: Parallelize key deserialization in SDK loops

💡 What: Replaced sequential `for...await` loops with `Promise.all` + `.map()` for key deserialization (`suite.kem.deserializePublicKey`) in `draft01.ts` and `original-reply-reader.ts`.
🎯 Why: Iterating over an array of cryptographic operations sequentially blocks the main thread longer than necessary, acting as a performance bottleneck when processing envelopes with multiple wraps.
📊 Impact: Expected to reduce total key deserialization time to roughly the duration of the slowest single operation instead of the sum of all operations, improving response/decryption speed.
🔬 Measurement: Can be verified by running the SDK test suite before and after on envelopes with large numbers of wraps and measuring the time spent in `openDraftEnvelope` or `readCurrent`.
