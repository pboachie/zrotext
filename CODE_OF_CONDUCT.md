# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Keep technical criticism specific to the work and its evidence.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an established private contact channel. Before opening the project to public contributions, maintainers must publish a monitored contact address and a documented response process. Maintainers may moderate or remove content and restrict participation to protect contributors.
