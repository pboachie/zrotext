# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Critique code and ideas with specific evidence, and assume good faith while discussing disagreements.

Report a conduct concern privately to `support@elydb.com` with “ZROtext conduct” in the subject. Include links and context if you can, but do not send someone else's sensitive information. A maintainer will review the report, may ask for more detail, and may remove content or restrict participation. Reports will be shared only with people needed to address them. There is no guaranteed response time yet.
